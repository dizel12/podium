# Code of Conduct

[한국어](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ko.md)

This is a short code of conduct, adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1 (CC BY 4.0). Anyone who opens an issue, sends a pull request, or joins a discussion is expected to follow it.

## Our standard

Be kind and be specific. We want podium to be easy to join for people of any background, experience level, language, and way of working.

Things that help: respectful, patient replies; criticism aimed at the work and not the person; saying what you tried and what happened; admitting mistakes and learning from them; accepting that English and Korean are both first-class here.

Things that are not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, sexualized comments or images, publishing someone's private information, repeated unwanted contact, and anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@stlahxm](https://github.com/stlahxm)) may edit, hide, or delete comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. We will try to explain the reason, and we will not promise a response time.

To report a problem privately, open a private report in this repository: **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct report". You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment itself. Reports are kept private and are handled by the maintainer.
